# Visual Style: Organic Brutalist (nature-inspired)

Raw, honest brutalist structure softened by earthy colour and organic shapes.

- Warm paper #F4F1E8; soil #1C1A17; moss #2F4A2B; sage #A9B99A;
  clay #C2694A; sand #E3D5B8. Flat fills, no gradients.
- Oversized, heavy headings; readable sans-serif body; monospace labels.
- Thick borders; rectangular grid mixed with arches, pills and leaf forms.
- Hard offset shadows; buttons shift to close their shadow gap on hover.
- Visible grids and full-width horizontal bands with asymmetric compositions.
- Real destination photos, botanical line decoration, minimal motion.
- Avoid glass, blurred shadows, neon colours and generic blue SaaS styling.
